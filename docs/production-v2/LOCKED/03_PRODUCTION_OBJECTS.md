# Production object presentation

## Speech Part

Speech Part is the most important repeated component. Stable regions: selection/order identity; Cast + Voice; exact method; script excerpt; selected Take summary; captions summary; accounting; operation/state lane; primary actions; overflow.

Always-visible creative/technical line: model family + tier + capability + language, e.g. `Qwen Audio · Flash · Expressive + tags · English`. Provider/region may remain secondary unless daily provider comparison makes promotion useful.

Cast Role = stable color + role name. Voice = avatar + Voice name. Without Cast, no fake role; use Voice and optional `Direct voice`.

Script comfortable default about four lines with Show more/collapse. Expansion must preserve scroll position.

Take summary example: `Take 3 selected · 00:12.4 · 4 Takes`. Input truth must expose `Original`, `Spoken` or `Tagged` used by selected Take.

Captions examples: `CC —`, `CC EN`, `CC EN + FR`, `CC Creating…`, `CC EN stale`, `CC Failed`; click opens Captions Workbench.

Orthogonal states can coexist: Draft, Ready, playing, Workbench-active, bulk-selected, queued, generating, retrying, confirmation, review/ambiguous, failed, lost, new unselected Take, selected Take outdated, fidelity warning, missing audio, captions none/generating/ready/stale/failed/translations, historical route unavailable. Do not collapse all into one status color.

Reserve a lower operation lane, e.g. `TAKE 4 · GENERATING 68%`, `WAITING FOR CONFIRMATION`, `REVIEW REQUIRED`, `FAILED`, `TAKE 4 READY · Listen / Use`. The rest of the Part does not reflow drastically.

Draft Speech shows saved text/context, Not recorded, Continue writing, Record, and zero duration until audio.

Silence is compact and inline: `— Silence · 2.5 sec —`; edit duration inline; never Voice/route/Take/provider cost.

Linked Asset shows asset identity, Venture source, version/link where useful, duration, Play, Replace, Open source, missing source. Never speech concepts.
