# Cast, Music and Player

Cast is optional. No Cast means no giant empty Cast card/reel. Header may keep Cast action.

When Cast exists, show compact horizontal Cast Reel above Sequence toolbar: role color, role name, Voice avatar/name. Do not make sticky by default. Click opens Cast Workbench. Hover may show Persona/Voice/Part count but important actions are not hover-only.

Role color is consistent in Cast Reel, Speech Part rail, search/filter, future Timeline blocks; always paired with text/avatar.

Cast Workbench uses existing capabilities and copy `Future recordings use the new voice. Existing Takes remain unchanged.` Cast Role does not choose hidden exact route.

Music Bed is a compact parallel lane. None: `Music Bed · None · + Add`. Present: source, audition, volume, offset, ducking, fades, Edit.

Music Workbench: source, audition, volume, offset/slip, ducking, fades, Replace, Remove.

A large temporary Asset Explorer modal is appropriate for Music/Venture audio browsing, upload, search and audition. Audition is distinct from selecting/inserting.

One global Player state. Production presentation is compact floating console centered over Canvas. Controls: title/context, play/pause, seek, elapsed/total, CC, speed, compact volume, download when durable, close.

If CC enabled and transcript exists, show current cue above transport. Clicking cue opens Part Captions Workbench. CC menu handles language choice without mutating canonical captions.

Playing Part gets distinct playing indicator. Player title may locate source Part. Production preview is clearly distinct and becomes stale when Production changes.
